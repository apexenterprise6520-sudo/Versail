About VERSAIL

VERSAIL began with one idea: clothes should adapt to your day, not the other way around. The name joins “versatile” with “sail”, a reminder that good design catches the wind and goes somewhere.

Honest fabrics

Heavy cotton, recycled nylon and wool blends chosen to last years, not seasons.

Clean cuts

Simple silhouettes with room to layer. Nothing loud, everything useful.

Small batches

We make fewer pieces, more carefully, and restock only what you wear most.
